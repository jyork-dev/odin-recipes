Guacamole

Description

Make this delicious creamy guacamole for your next party or taco night!

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 ripe tomato
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Mash avocado in a medium serving bowl.
  3. Stir in onion, tomato, and garlic.
  4. Season with lime juice and salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Cover and chill guacamole for 30 minutes to allow flavors to blen.
